## Pricing Formula Reference

### Per-Kilogram Pricing (₹/kg)
Used for: Weight-based products (coconut powders, oils)

**Formula:** `(grams ÷ 1000) × ₹rate_per_kg`

**Example**: Desiccated Coconut Powder Low Fat @ ₹170/kg
- 500g: (500 ÷ 1000) × 170 = **₹85**
- 1kg: (1000 ÷ 1000) × 170 = **₹170**
- 5kg: (5000 ÷ 1000) × 170 = **₹850**

### Per-100ml Pricing (₹/100ml)
Used for: Oil and liquid products

**Formula:** `(milliliters ÷ 100) × ₹rate_per_100ml`

**Example**: Baby Care Coconut Oil @ ₹180/100ml
- 50ml: (50 ÷ 100) × 180 = **₹90**
- 100ml: (100 ÷ 100) × 180 = **₹180**
- 500ml: (500 ÷ 100) × 180 = **₹900**

### Fixed Pricing (₹/bottle or ₹/unit)
Used for: Pre-packaged products (capsules, bars)

**Formula:** `Direct fixed amount in ₹`

**Example**: VCO Softgel Capsules @ ₹384/bottle
- 60 capsules: **₹384** (fixed price, no calculation)

## Measurement Standard Conversion

### Weight Units (Grams)
- 500g = 0.5 kg
- 1kg = 1,000g
- 5kg = 5,000g
- Always display as: "500g", "1kg", "5kg" (not "0.5kg")

### Volume Units (Milliliters)
- 250ml = 0.25L
- 500ml = 0.5L
- 1L = 1,000ml
- Always display as: "250ml", "500ml", "1L" (not "0.25L")
- For oils by weight: "≈900ml" (approximate, since density ≈ 0.9)

### Special Cases
- VCO Softgel Capsules: Counted as "60 capsules" (not ml or g)
- Coconut Soap: "Natural bar" (exact weight TBD)

## Important Notes

1. **All prices in ₹ (Indian Rupees)** - No rupee signs (?) used
2. **Exact measurements** - All variants show exact g or ml amounts
3. **Price calculations** - Unit rate × quantity automatically calculated
4. **Rounding** - Per-unit prices rounded to nearest rupee
5. **No decimals** - Prices shown as whole rupee amounts (₹85, not ₹85.50)
6. **Comma separators** - Used for thousands (₹1,400 not ₹1400)
7. **Approximate volumes** - Oils marked with "≈" (e.g., ≈900ml) when weight-based
