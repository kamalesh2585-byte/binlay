## Key Specifications

### Unit Pricing Models Used

1. **Per-Kilogram (₹/kg)**: Products 1, 2, 7, 12
   - Formula: (grams ÷ 1000) × rate = price in ₹
   - Used for: Powders, bulk oils

2. **Per-100ml (₹/100ml)**: Products 9, 10, 11
   - Formula: (milliliters ÷ 100) × rate = price in ₹
   - Used for: Oils, liquid products in bottles

3. **Fixed Price (₹/bottle)**: Product 8
   - Direct fixed amount, no calculation
   - Used for: Capsule bottles, pre-packaged items

4. **TBD**: Products 3, 4, 5, 6, 13
   - Pricing to be determined and confirmed

5. **Request Quote**: Product 14
   - Custom orders, pricing varies by request
   - Used for: Customizable products (soap)

### Measurement Standards

- **Weight**: Always in grams (g) or kilograms (kg notation for "1kg", "5kg")
- **Volume**: Always in milliliters (ml) or liters (L notation for "1L", "5L")
- **Capsule Count**: Counted in units (e.g., "60 capsules")
- **Approximations**: Prefixed with "≈" when density conversion used (oils)

### Price Display Standards

- **Rupee Sign**: ₹ (no ? or "Rs.")
- **Formatting**: Whole rupee amounts (₹85, not ₹85.00 or ₹85.50)
- **Thousands**: Comma separator (₹1,400 not ₹1400)
- **TBD Products**: Display "TBD" in price field
- **Quote Products**: Display "Request Quote" button
